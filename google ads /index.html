Frequently Asked Questions

What types of gifts does The Gift Edit offer?

▼
The Gift Edit specializes in aesthetic and personalised gifting: custom initial drop pendants (18K gold finish), solid oak Spotify wooden memory frames, multi-pair artisan Meenakari jhumka velvet organisers, pure mulberry silk hair accessories, and lavish jewellery & confectionery gift hampers.

Can I personalise a gift?

▼
Yes! You can choose two custom initial letters (A–Z) for our Initial Drop Pendant, supply your photo, favorite track, and dedication text for the Spotify Wooden Frame, and include handwritten wax-sealed calligraphy notes with any hamper.

What gifts are suitable for a best friend?

▼
For best friends, our most viral picks are the "Sweet Treats & Sparkle" Jewellery Hamper (₹1,599), the Spotify Wooden Memory Frame (₹699) capturing your shared anthem, and the Mulberry Silk Scrunchie & Daisy Hairpin Set (₹349).

Can I find gifts within my budget?

▼
Yes, our collection is curated for realistic student and young adult budgets. Accessories begin at ₹349, personalised pendants and jhumkas from ₹449–₹499, memory frames at ₹699, and full multi-sensory hampers from ₹1,299 to ₹1,799.

How can I order a gift hamper?

▼
You can click any "Order on WhatsApp" button to connect instantly with pre-filled item details, or use our "Build Your Own Gift Box" wizard to handpick items, specify your budget, and submit your custom order summary.

What are the available delivery options?

▼
All orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours across India. Delivery typically takes 3 to 6 business days depending on your location. Safe protective packaging is used for all fragile frames and confectionery treats.
